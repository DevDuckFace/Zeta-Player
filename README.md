# Zeta Player
![image alt](https://github.com/DevDuckFace/Zeta-Player/blob/df2373cc1725610d10d5bcc00a51e2287dbc62ea/Screenshot_1.png)
A modern, all-in-one desktop app to watch, search and download videos, lives and playlists — with a clean interface and a built-in video player. No browser, no clutter, just one window.

## Download

**[⬇ Download Zeta Player](https://github.com/DevDuckFace/Zeta-Player/releases/download/zeta2.0/ZetaPlayerSetup.exe)**

Windows 64-bit. Run the installer and you're ready to go — everything the app needs is bundled inside.

## Features

- **Watch videos instantly** in the built-in player, with playback controls, seek bar and volume.
- **Choose your playback quality** — from 360p all the way up to 4K, or let it always pick the maximum. The player shows the quality it's currently streaming.
- **Download videos** in the quality you want, or extract audio only.
- **Record live streams** and save them to your computer.
- **Search videos** with results that keep loading as you scroll — no limits — each with its thumbnail.
- **Playlists tab** — search playlists, open any of them to browse the videos inside, and download single videos or the whole playlist at once.
- **Channels tab** — search channels, save your favorites, and open a channel to see all of its videos. Sort them by most recent, most viewed or oldest.
- **Log in with your browser** so private or restricted content works, using your existing session.
- **Pick where downloads are saved**, or have the app ask you every time.
- **English and Portuguese (Brazil)** interface.
